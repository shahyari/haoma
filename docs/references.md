### Policy compliance review (2026-11-14) — owner decisions needed

These `Approved` rules rest on sources that the 2026-11-14 policy places outside its preferred tiers. `science.md` says a claim found weaker than recorded goes back to `Unverified`, but the owner approved these rules today. **Conflict: Claude has not changed any status.** The owner decides each item.

- **C-1 Temperature (RULE-14, RULE-15, RULE-17) — RESOLVED (OD-11):** the 37.5 °C and 39.4 °C values and the fluids-and-rest advice come only from Cleveland Clinic (REF-31, 2b). Research 2026-11-14: Mayo Clinic's fever page (https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/fever/symptoms-causes/syc-20352759) blocks automated checks, so its wording is unknown. MedlinePlus (REF-50, NLM) states all three points: adult oral fever "above 99°F to 99.5°F (37.2°C to 37.5°C)", call if a fever "stays at or keeps rising above 103°F (39.4°C)", and "Drink fluids and rest". It does not call 37.5–37.9 °C "low-grade". No guideline-tier (2a) source was found for these adult home values. Options: (a) the owner exports the Mayo page so Claude can check it; (b) approve MedlinePlus as a 2b source; (c) approve Cleveland Clinic as a 2b source.
- **C-2 Hypoglycemia (RULE-09, RULE-11, RULE-12) — RESOLVED (OD-12):** REF-25 defines levels for people with diabetes. Options: keep them, labeled "defined for people with diabetes"; find a source for people without diabetes; or turn the rules into team decisions.
  - Research 2026-11-14: NASA OSDR (https://science.nasa.gov/biological-physical/data/osdr/) holds omics and biology datasets, not clinical glucose thresholds. The 43 "glucose" study hits (cgene, alsda; the esa source returned none) are microbe, plant, animal or cell-culture studies. OSD-936 is human HEK293 cells (mitochondrial translation). The only human in-vivo study, OSD-370 (REF-52), is a bed-rest analog showing insulin resistance, which is useful context but not a threshold source.
  - The Endocrine Society guideline (REF-53, 2a) covers adults **without** diabetes. It recommends evaluating hypoglycemia only when Whipple's triad is documented: symptoms, a low reading, and symptoms resolving once glucose is raised. A low number without symptoms is therefore not hypoglycemia in this population.
- **C-3 Fasting glucose (RULE-09, RULE-10, RULE-13) — RESOLVED (OD-11):** REF-24 is a textbook summary (2b). Checked 2026-11-14: REF-46 (ADA, 2a) states both values. The values do not change.
- **C-4 PHQ-2 (RULE-21–RULE-23) — RESOLVED (OD-11):** REF-40 is a university summary page (2b). Found 2026-11-14: the original validation study (REF-51, Kroenke 2003, 2a) supports RULE-23 (≥ 3: sensitivity 83%, specificity 92%, "optimal cutpoint"). Its abstract does **not** support RULE-22's "cutoff of 2 is more sensitive". That claim rests on REF-40 only until the full text is checked. Proposal: cite REF-51 for RULE-23; for RULE-22, keep REF-40 (needs approval as a 2b source) or label the score-2 threshold a team decision.
