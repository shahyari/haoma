## Information architecture

Owner brainstorm Section 16 (2026-11-14):

- **Header** (owner, 2026-11-15): on the left the logo with the name "Haoma" (capitalised in the interface), linking to Home, and the "Demo data" chip next to it; on the right the primary **Log entry** button (icon only below 640 px, name kept for screen readers) and the avatar. Log entry is in the header on every page. The avatar on the right opens the **avatar card** (Section 17): avatar, "Alex Morgan" (`text-lg` semibold, larger than the role, owner 2026-11-15), "Fictional Flight Engineer" (`text-sm`); links Reports, Settings, About; a dashed box "Demo data and fictional person — All health measurements in the demo are simulated." The demo controls moved to Settings → **Demo controls** (owner usability review, 2026-11-15), so "Clear data" is not one click away from "Reports". The simulated demo history loads automatically on the first visit (owner 2026-11-14); after Clear data it is not reloaded until the user chooses Load demo data. Mission name, mission day, and the "indicators to review" count are no longer shown, per the wireframe. The "Demo data" chip next to the logo replaces the demo disclaimer on the main screen. It is the shadcn Badge, secondary variant, and a button that opens the demo notice in a popover, with a link to Settings (owner consistency review, 2026-11-15). There is no separate navigation bar.
- **Home:** six separate indicator cards (Heart rate, Blood pressure, Blood glucose, Body temperature, Sleep, Mood) in a left column below a **summary strip** (owner usability review, 2026-11-15; details below), the body-figure area in the middle, and a right column with the **activity graph** at the top and the status banners and the RULE-30 reminder below it when they appear (owner, 2026-11-15, giving the cards more vertical room). **Column widths** (owner, 2026-11-15): from 1280 px a 4-column grid split 1 : 2 : 1, so the cards and the right column are always equal and the body map is twice as wide; from 1024 to 1279 px a 3-column grid split 1 : 2 (cards, body map), with the right column full width below; one column below 1024 px. Below 1280 px the right column moves under the cards and the figure; on narrow screens the order is cards, figure, activity graph, banners. Only Home stretches to the full screen width; **every other page (indicator history, Reports, a report, Settings, About, not found) keeps the earlier fixed width** (max 1024 px, centered) and start with an outline **Back** button (previous page in the app, or Home when opened directly) (owner, 2026-11-14). They all use one layout, `PageLayout` (owner, 2026-11-15): max 1024 px, centred, content at the full width of that column; 12 px between Back and the title row, 32 px between sections, 12 px between a section heading (`text-lg`) and its card, and 16 px card padding.
- **Indicator card** (owner 2026-11-14): header row with icon, name, and the status badge at the top right (vertically centred with the heading); latest value; any rule wording such as "Below general sleep recommendation" as text below the value; then "Measured 5 mins ago" / "Measured 2 hours ago" for a reading taken today (for mood, today's daily rating), or "Not measured today · last yesterday" / "… last 3 days ago" (the full timestamp is kept for screen readers only), followed by input context and "Simulated"; the PHQ-2 screening state on the Mood card. The whole card links to the indicator's history page, where every reading has its full timestamp.
- **Values and units** (owner consistency review, 2026-11-15): wherever a reading is shown with its unit (card, latest-measurement panel, banner), the number is monospaced (unless "Monospaced numbers" is turned off in Settings; digits stay equal-width) and the unit (and a leading word such as "Rating") is in the normal font, smaller (half the number size, at least 14 px), with a real space between them.
- **Due marker** (owner usability review, 2026-11-15): a card with no reading today shows a neutral dashed "Due" chip before the badge. It is not a badge and has no status colour. The badge keeps the latest reading's status: readings do not expire (AU-6), and check-in completeness stays separate from status. So a card can show "Due" next to "Normal".
- **Activity graph** (owner usability review, 2026-11-15): a contribution-calendar grid at the top of the right column (moved from the cards column, owner 2026-11-15), at most 320 px wide, or the full right-column width from 1280 px. There are 12 Monday-start weeks, with today outlined, and one cell per day. A cell gets darker as more indicators are logged that day: 0, 1–2, 3–4, 5, or all 6 (one hue, never a status colour). Mood counts the daily rating only. Each cell has a tooltip and an accessible label ("14 Nov 2026: 6 of 6 indicators logged"). The graph shows logging activity, not health.
- **Log entry** (owner usability review, 2026-11-15; summary strip removed and the button moved to the header, owner 2026-11-15): Log entry opens a dialog with six tabs in card order and starts on the first due indicator. The person fills in one or more tabs and saves them together ("Save 2 entries"), and each tab becomes its own entry with the current time. A filled tab shows a check; a tab with errors shows an alert icon. If any filled tab is invalid, nothing is saved, and the dialog names the tabs to check and opens the first one. "Add entry" on the history page is unchanged.
- **Status badges** (owner 2026-11-14): the shadcn Badge at its default size and type (outline variant, `text-xs`), fully rounded. A badge only ever says **Normal**, **Attention**, or **Escalate** (status colour), or the neutral **Not evaluated** / **No measurement**. Any other wording (for example "Below general sleep recommendation", "Below screening cutoff", "Further assessment recommended", "Screening due", "Not assessed") is written next to the badge: on cards and in the latest-measurement panel as text, in banners under the title, in the rules list as "Shown as “…”", and for mood in the PHQ-2 screening line.
- **Back button** (owner 2026-11-14): an outline button labelled "Back" on every page except Home (indicator history, Reports, a report, Settings, About).
- **History entries are editable** (owner, 2026-11-14): each table row has an Edit button that opens the entry form with the stored values. The measurement time stays the same; the edited entry becomes a manual entry marked "(edited)" and is re-evaluated. A PHQ-2 is edited by answering both items again, because only the total is stored.
- **History table pagination and delete** (owner, 2026-11-15): the table shows the newest readings first, 10 per page by default, with "Rows per page" (10, 25, 50), the range ("1–10 of 15"), and Previous / Next below the table. Each row has two icon buttons, Edit (pencil) and Delete (bin), with hover titles and full accessible names ("Delete reading from 15 Nov 2026, 07:00"). Delete opens a confirmation dialog naming the reading ("This cannot be undone. The status, baseline, and history are recalculated without it."), with Cancel and a destructive "Delete reading". Deleting a demo reading stops the daily demo refresh, so it does not come back.
- **Indicator icons** (Lucide, approved by the owner 2026-11-14; blood pressure follows the owner's example): heart rate HeartPulse, blood pressure Droplet, glucose Pipette, temperature Thermometer, sleep Moon, mood Smile.

## Display settings (owner brainstorm, Sections 14 and 17, 2026-11-14)

**Settings page (Section 17):** cards with divided rows. Every row follows one pattern (owner, 2026-11-15): title (`text-sm` medium), optional hint (`text-sm` muted), then the control, 8 px apart, with 16 px padding; radio and checkbox rows keep 36 px touch targets without adding visible space. **Appearances**: Theme, Mode, Contrast, Text size, **Monospaced numbers** (checkbox, on by default, with a 118/76 example; replaces the note at the bottom of Settings, owner 2026-11-15), Assistive patterns. **Units and Formats**: Temperature, Measurement system, Date format. **Demo controls** (owner usability review, 2026-11-15): Mission zone (Earth, Space, Moon, Mars; drives the automatic theme), Mission phase (drives RULE-26 and RULE-30), **Load demo data** and **Clear data**. Both ask for confirmation in a dialog (owner, 2026-11-15; replaces the second click). The dialog states what is deleted (with counts) and what is kept, and offers Cancel and a destructive action. Clear data: "Delete all data?" (entries, pain logs, and pain reports are deleted; the mission phase is reset to In flight; display settings are kept; the demo data is not reloaded until chosen; download reports first). Load demo data, only when entries exist: "Replace your entries with demo data?" (entries are replaced; pain logs, reports, and settings are kept). Rule texts keep the units of their sources (°C, mg/dL); the rules section says so when other units are chosen.

## Body map (F-04), owner prototype (2026-11-14)

The owner created the body map in the Claude app (`body-map.html`) and asked for it in the Home body-figure area to save reports of pain and issues. It replaces the earlier navigation proposal (chest, abdomen, head, whole body → indicators), which is not built.

- **3D view** (default when WebGL is available): drag to rotate, Shift-drag or right-drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, click to add a pain point; Front, Back, L, R presets; Zoom and Reset buttons at the bottom left of the figure. The **3D/2D toggle** sits at the bottom right of the figure, mirroring them, in the same button style (owner 2026-11-14). Numbered markers sit on the surface with leader labels; markers hidden behind the body are dimmed.
- **2D view:** front and back outlines split into named regions, plus 12 organs on the front. Every region and organ is a button reachable with Tab; Enter or Space adds a point. This is the keyboard path, and it is used automatically when WebGL is missing.
- **Pain log** (beside the stage on wide containers, below it on narrow ones): Points and Avg /10 cards (the Max card was removed, owner 2026-11-14); each log with number, region, character, surface (front, back, or organ), time, intensity bar, note, and related indicators; actions Show on body, Edit, Delete; **Save report**; Clear all with a confirmation step.
- **Dialog** (app dialog component): region as the title, related indicators, intensity 1–10 (number keys work; 0 = 10), character chips (Aching, Sharp, Throbbing, Burning, Cramping, Tingling, Pressure), then **date and time of the pain** (below Character, owner 2026-11-14): the shadcn date picker (Calendar in a Popover; days after today disabled) and a shadcn time input (browser picker icon hidden, as in the shadcn date-time example); defaults to now; future times are rejected. Then the note. Editing also offers Delete.
- **Related indicators** (owner 2026-11-14, "wherever possible"): front chest regions and the heart → Heart rate, Blood pressure; front abdomen regions, liver, stomach, intestines → Blood glucose; head, face, back of head, brain → Sleep, Mood; every region → Body temperature (whole body). Links only; a pain log says nothing about the linked indicator. Other regions (limbs, back, eyes, ears, lungs, kidneys, bladder) have only the whole-body link (`src/features/bodymap/links.ts`).
- **Reports** (owner 2026-11-14): "Save report" saves one or more chosen logs (all ticked by default) as a report and **removes them from the pain log**; unticked logs stay. A temporary banner ("Report saved with N pain logs") with a **View report** button appears for 8 seconds at the bottom of the pain log (owner 2026-11-14). The **Reports** page (avatar menu, and the Reports button at the top right of the body map) lists reports newest first with the astronaut name, number of logs, and average intensity; each has View report, Open JSON (new tab), Download JSON, and Delete (with confirmation). **Import JSON** reads a report file (schema `haoma.painReport.v1`, max 1 MB); everything is validated and nothing is imported if any part is invalid; a duplicate report id gets a new id.
- **Report view** (`/reports/:id`): the report's logs on the body map, read-only (3D/2D, show on body; no adding, editing, or deleting; 2D regions are not interactive), with the same actions as the list.
- **Report file (JSON):** `schema`, `id`, `astronaut` (name at the time of saving), `savedAt`, a "not a diagnosis" note, and `logs`.
- **Size:** on wide screens the body map fills the viewport height below the header and the width up to the banner column; the right column (activity graph, banners) is always present. When the card is too narrow for the pain log beside the figure (under 768 px of card width, e.g. at 1024 px on Home or on the report page), the figure and pain log stack and scroll inside the card, so the hint row ("Drag to rotate · …") always stays in its own row at the bottom (bug fix, 2026-11-15).
- **Integration changes from the prototype (Claude):** the white-on-black HUD is mapped to theme tokens, so it follows the theme, light or dark mode, and contrast settings. The panel and dialog use the app's components. The prototype's Google Fonts are not loaded; the app fonts are used. Pulse animations are off with reduced motion. Reports are stored with the app's data, not under a separate key.
- **Wording:** "Pain log · self-reported, not a diagnostic tool". Organ names describe where the person points; nothing is interpreted, and no status or rule uses the reports.

## Crew comparison (owner spec 2026-11-15)

On each indicator's history page, after History and before the rules (owner, 2026-11-15).

- **Summary card** "Crew comparison" with a secondary badge **"Simulated crew data"**. It covers the latest comparable reading (for mood, the latest daily rating) and shows these rows: Your latest measurement (with time), Crew average, Difference (signed, with unit; blood pressure as systolic / diastolic), Based on N other crew members, Period ("15 Nov 2026 (same mission day)"), and Compared readings (the comparability group, for example "At rest readings"). One neutral sentence per value follows: "Your reading is above / below / close to the crew average", with systolic and diastolic separately. Then a note on crew readings left out that day, with counts and reasons only. The fixed explanation reads: "Crew averages provide mission context, not medical reference ranges. Similar measurements across the crew do not necessarily indicate that they are safe." A disclosure, "How the crew average is calculated", explains which readings are included, why some are excluded, the minimum cohort, and why crew averages don't replace the personal baseline or a clinical assessment. No status badge and no status colour.
- **Unavailable state:** "Crew average not available." with the reason (reading not comparable, no comparable crew reading, or fewer than 3 other members). No numbers are shown.
- **Chart:** a checkbox "Show crew average (simulated)" next to the History heading (on by default) adds a crew-average line per own series ("Crew average", or "Crew average · Systolic" and so on). **Line styles (owner, 2026-11-15), for every history chart:** your readings are solid lines in the primary chart colour (`--chart-1`), told apart only by their markers: the first series (for example Systolic, Oral) has filled circles, the second (Diastolic, Forehead sensor) outlined circles, and the third (Method not recorded) outlined squares. Crew averages are dash-dot lines in the secondary chart colour (`--chart-2`) without markers, with gaps where no average exists. The legend and the hover tooltip show a sample of each line (line pattern, colour and marker as on the chart), so your readings and the crew average differ by line style, not colour alone (owner, 2026-11-15). The tooltip shows both values and "n = N". The caption names the dash-dot line. The table gets "Crew average (unit)" and "Crew members" columns ("Not available" / "–").
- **Privacy:** aggregates only. No crew names, no individual readings, and the average is hidden below 3 contributing members.

## Health communication

- Never rely on color alone for attention states. This also applies to the optional color coding (`challenge.md` O-05).
- Clearly distinguish missing, normal-for-baseline, and needs-review states. Define the labels only after science review (`science.md`).
- Show measurement units and input context.
- Mark demo and simulated data visibly.
- Explain uncertainty and avoid false precision.
- Never present unvalidated diagnostic conclusions as fact. Each guidance item shows its evidence-type label (Spaceflight evidence / General clinical reference / Team design decision / Simulated demo logic). A rule shows separate labels for its threshold basis and its action wording when they differ, per `science.md` → "Guidance policy".
- Escalation prompts name the recipient (crew medical officer or flight surgeons) and the readings that triggered them.
